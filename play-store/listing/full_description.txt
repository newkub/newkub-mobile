new-habbit is a beautiful lock-screen style clock app for Android and PWA. It helps you start the day and stay focused with five essential tools:

- Alarm: set recurring alarms, choose from built-in sounds or generate a custom AI voice with ElevenLabs.
- Stopwatch: track laps with best/worst lap highlights and easy share.
- Timer: use quick presets or create your own with live circle progress.
- Pomodoro: focus sessions, short & long breaks, and daily cycle stats.
- Reminder: schedule one-time or recurring reminders months ahead.

Key features:
- Cloud sync across devices via Cloudflare D1.
- Home screen clock widget.
- Lock screen style interface.
- Onboarding and notification setup.
- Works offline, syncs when online.
